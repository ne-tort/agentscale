Land M04E candidate reader, upstream gate and proof ports

Three of the four M04E ports are implemented over real tables: a bounded keyset
reader whose SQL and Python cursor orders agree exactly, a gate that re-proves
MIG-M04-01/02 and the typed parent chain per partition, and a proof port whose
output is validated by the consumer's own validator rather than by its own
assertions.

The fourth port refuses by name. EvidenceStore needs a per-source ledger,
observation digests, a durable cursor and audit; none of the three registered
M04E tables can hold them, and the promotion table's outcome CHECK admits only
blocked or quarantined. Returning None from find_existing would permit a second
row for an already-mapped source, which is exactly what the contract forbids --
so the port names the gap and the required DDL instead of inventing schema.
